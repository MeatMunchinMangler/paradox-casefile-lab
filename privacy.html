WHAT THIS EXPERIMENT DOES

PARADOX is a small browser mystery game. It has no account creation, advertising SDKs, user analytics, personal profiles, chat or payment processing. The case library is fictional.

Progress stays in your browser.

Completed cases, best scores and daily streak dates are saved only to this browser's localStorage. You can clear them with RESET in the game or your browser's site-data settings. We do not sync this progress to a server.

Sharing is your decision.

The share button prepares a case link and a spoiler-free result summary. It uses your device's native sharing tools when available. Nothing is automatically sent to another person.

Web hosting may keep access logs.

If this experiment is served through a third-party preview host, that provider may process standard connection data such as IP addresses, timestamps and requested URLs under its own platform terms. We do not add app-level tracking or request personal details. Do not treat a preview link as a secure messaging service.

Experimental status

This is an unmonetized, early playtest, not a subscription or a verified customer service. The 12 daily cases rotate; new stories are not promised every day. This description applies to experiment version 0.1 as of November 11, 2026.
