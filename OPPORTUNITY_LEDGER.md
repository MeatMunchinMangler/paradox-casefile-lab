# PARADOX opportunity experiment — evidence and decisions

As of 2026-11-11 (Pacific). Hypothesis, **not** demand or revenue validation.

## Why this experiment rather than another generic lead intake or social planner?

We looked at diverse consumer categories instead of relying solely on
previously developed HVAC products. A small, repeatable, visually obvious
mystery format can be developed, tested, and shown without subscriptions,
model credits, databases, accounts, or a large installed audience.

## Differentiated testable hook

**'Every case hides two statements that cannot both be true.'**

The player should understand the promise in under 10 seconds. Each case
offers a 45–120-second suspense loop, one correct pair, two hints, and a
spoiler-free share challenge. The app is useful without AI being available.
The artwork is CSS and original text, with no licensing dependency.

The exact brand 'PARADOX' is a **working title**, unverified for trademark,
domain or App Store availability. Do not make commercial name exclusivity claims.

## Demand validation experiment — explicitly opt-in

A proposed playtest (not outreach authorization): invite 15–20 consenting
people through an approved distribution channel after a reviewed public
preview exists. Ask for **behavior** rather than compliments: did they
finish a case, tap a second case, send the spoiler-free challenge on their
own, and return the next day? Collect only opt-in aggregate observations,
not contact data or sensitive personal information.

For a first go/no-go:
- At least 10 players complete one mystery without assistance.
- At least 4 of 15 voluntarily play a second mystery.
- At least 3 voluntary spoiler-free shares from 15 testers.
- A few players ask for more cases without prompting.

These numbers are **experimental decision thresholds**, not forecasts or
claims that the market values the game. Failure means learn, revise or
retire—not automatically expand scope.

If genuine repeat use appears, only then consider an optional one-time
premium case pack, perhaps $3.99, with a licensed payment solution and
clear terms. That price is an untested idea. Avoid fake checkout controls,
upfront subscriptions or paid acquisition before player evidence.

## Ownership, safety and continuity

- Project source: isolated repo in owner's Documents/Moonshot App Lab.
- No GitHub push, hosted domain, money, external messaging, or unrelated
  modifications happened during the initial build.
- No personal chat archive, consumer identity, account connector, nor
  third-party model calls are needed for gameplay.
- The Moonshot App Lab automation can revisit this evidence and compare it
  to other opportunities. It should not spend 20 cycles polishing a game
  without any observed player demand.
- Product status labels: `LOCAL_PLAYABLE`, `HOSTED_PREVIEW_PENDING`,
  `DEMAND_UNVALIDATED`, `REVENUE_ZERO`.

Recognition -> integrate evidence -> build -> observe actual use ->
correct -> expand only with deserved demand. Joy is law.
